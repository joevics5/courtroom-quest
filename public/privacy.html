Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Courtroom Quest is a game where you argue fictional legal cases against AI and other players. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account: if you sign up, your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider). If you play as a guest, we create an anonymous account with no email. Profile: your nickname, avatar, rank and win count. Your nickname, avatar, rank and wins can be seen by other players (for example on leaderboards and in two-player games). Your email is never shown to other players.

Gameplay: the cases you play, your questions and arguments, transcripts, verdicts, and your game settings. Shared transcripts and live-watch features make a game visible to others only when you use those features.

Custom cases: if you paste a case story or upload a PDF to build a case, the content is sent to our AI provider to create the case. Uploaded PDFs are deleted after processing (or by automatic cleanup if processing is interrupted). Do not upload documents containing real people's confidential information.

Voice: if you use voice input, your browser's built-in speech recognition handles your audio; depending on your browser this may be processed by the browser vendor (for example Google or Apple). We receive only the resulting text. Spoken court audio is generated on your device.

Technical: basic logs (such as request times and errors) kept by our hosting and database providers to run and secure the service.

How we use it

To run the game, show your progress and rankings, match you with opponents, prevent abuse (for example rate-limiting AI use), and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

AI processing

Game text (case details, your questions and arguments, and recent conversation) is sent to Google's Gemini models to generate witness answers, opposing counsel, rulings and verdicts. Please do not type personal or sensitive information about yourself or others into the game.

Who processes your data

Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions), Google (AI generation), and our web host. They process data on our behalf to provide the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and game history until you ask us to delete them. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address below and we will remove it. Anonymous guest accounts that are no longer used may be removed periodically.

Children

Courtroom Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the game.
